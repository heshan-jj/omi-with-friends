# Omi with friends

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User approved Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Motion, PartyServer on Cloudflare Durable Objects (successor to the discontinued hosted PartyKit service), Vitest, Playwright, GitHub and Vercel. Develop in the cloud.

## Users

The owner and their friends, playing the Sri Lankan partnership card game Omi from phones and computers.

## Product Purpose

Create a private room, share its link, choose four seats, and play a complete match together.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Guest nicknames and browser-held reconnect credentials. Four human players; bots and public matchmaking deferred. Server-authoritative dealing and moves. Hands remain private. Persist games and pause during disconnection. Default 10-token scoring with carry after drawn hands. Kapothi announcement variation remains an open decision; initial rules use automatic three-token sweeps.

## Product Principles

Make joining easy. Prioritize readable cards and clear state. Preserve the game across reconnections. Never expose opponents' hands.
